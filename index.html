Capitales

Each question shows one country's outline. Name its capital from four cities, or place the capital yourself on the map, against a 15-second clock, in English or French. A remake of a Visual Basic game from about 1996. Each version below was built by a different AI assistant.

Claude version

Two modes, Name and Place. In Place, distance is scored against the size of the map, so 57 km out costs more in Switzerland than in Canada. Ten questions a game, streak multipliers, and a top ten for each mode, kept in your browser.
